Seaplane probe

← Back to Seaplane. Reads every setting from a ROLI Seaboard M / Seaboard Block over MIDI. This page only reads. It does not change anything stored on the keyboard.

This browser can't talk to MIDI devices

Open this page in Firefox or Chrome. Safari doesn't support Web MIDI.

1. Connect

  1. Plug the Seaboard in with USB-C and switch it on. Quit ROLI Dashboard or anything else that might be talking to it.
  2. Click Allow MIDI access. Your browser will ask for permission to use MIDI devices, including "system exclusive" messages. Allow it.
  3. Check the two ports below are the Seaboard, then click Connect and read settings.
